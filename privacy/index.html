Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

Jolly Racer is made by Daniel Jooryabi ("we"). This policy explains what the game collects, why, and the choices you have. The short version: there are no accounts and no ads. The game collects anonymous usage and performance data to help us improve it, and we don't use it for advertising.

What we don't collect or do

We don't ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or advertising identifier. There's no sign-in and no account. We don't sell data, share it with advertisers or data brokers, or combine it with data from other companies to track you across their apps or websites.

Usage and performance data

The game collects general information about how it's played and how it performs, along with basic information about the device, such as its model, operating system and approximate region. This is tied to an anonymous ID, not to your identity or your advertising identifier. We use it to understand how the game is played, make it better and fix problems.

This data is collected and processed for us by Unity Technologies; see Unity's privacy policy for how Unity handles it.

Data kept on your device

Your scores, settings and game progress are saved on your device and aren't sent to us.

In-app purchases

Purchases are made through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details or Apple ID. We only learn that a purchase was made, so we can give you the item.

How long we keep it

Usage and performance data is kept only as long as it's useful for improving the game.

Your choices

Deleting the game removes everything it saved on your device, and a new install gets a new anonymous ID. To ask us to delete the data sent from your device, or for a copy of it, email us at the address below. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK or California) you may have further rights under local law, and you can contact us to use them.

Children

Jolly Racer isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it on this page and change the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email jollyracerapp@gmail.com.
