Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

Jolly Racer is made by Daniel Jooryabi ("we"). This policy explains what the game collects, why, and the choices you have. The short version: there are no accounts, no ads and no tracking. The game sends anonymous gameplay statistics so we can tune how hard it is, and that's all.

What we don't collect

We don't ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or advertising identifier. There's no sign-in and no account. We don't sell data, and we don't use it to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Gameplay statistics (Unity Analytics)

The game uses Unity Analytics, a service of Unity Technologies, to send a short summary of each run: for example how long it lasted, the score, what you crashed into, how many coins you collected, whether you finished the tutorial, what you bought at the in-game mechanic shop, the control method (touch, keyboard or controller) and the frame rate. We use it to find the parts of the game that are too hard, too easy or annoying, and to fix performance problems.

Along with these events, Unity's service records technical information about the device: an anonymous ID created when the game is first installed, the device model, operating system and version, the game's version, language and region settings, and a country estimated from the internet connection. This ID is not your advertising identifier and isn't linked to your identity. Unity processes this data on our behalf; see Unity's privacy policy for how Unity handles it.

Data kept on your device

Your best score, coins, settings and tutorial progress are saved on your device. The game also writes a detailed play log of each run to its own folder on your device, which you can see in the Files app (iPhone and iPad) and delete there. None of this is sent to us.

In-app purchases

Purchases are made through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details or Apple ID. We only learn that a purchase was made, so we can give you the item.

How long we keep it

Gameplay statistics are kept only as long as they're useful for improving the game, and no longer than Unity Analytics' retention period.

Your choices

Deleting the game removes everything it saved on your device, and a new install gets a new anonymous ID. To ask us to delete the statistics sent from your device, or for a copy of them, email us at the address below with your device model and roughly when you played, and we'll work with Unity to handle the request. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK or California) you may have further rights under local law, and you can contact us to use them.

Children

Jolly Racer isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it on this page and change the date at the top.

Contact
