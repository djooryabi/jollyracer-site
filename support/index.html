Support

Stuck in traffic? Email jollyracerapp@gmail.com and we'll get back to you, usually within a few days. Tell us your device (for example "iPhone 15" or "MacBook Air"), the version of the game (on the App Store page), and what happened.

How do I play?

On iPhone and iPad, tilt the device like a steering wheel to steer, swipe up to jump with the spring, and swipe down and hold to brake. On a Mac, steer with the arrow keys or A and D, press F to jump and hold Space to brake. A game controller works on every device. You can replay the tutorial any time with How to play on the home screen.

I bought an extra life. Where is it?

The extra life is a heart that's added at the mechanic shop. Bought in the shop, it's added right away. If a purchase finished while the game was closed, or was waiting for approval (Ask to Buy), the heart is added the next time you visit the mechanic shop: take an exit marked SHOP, and it's already paid for.

If it still doesn't show up, email us with the date of the purchase. For a refund, use Apple's Report a Problem page.

Does the game need an internet connection?

No. You can play anywhere. Buying an extra life needs a connection to the App Store.

How do I reset my best score?

Your best score and settings are saved on your device. Deleting the app and installing it again starts you fresh, tutorial included.

The game runs slowly or the screen moves too much.

Close other apps and make sure Low Power Mode is off. Under Settings in the game you can change the tilt steering sensitivity, and on a Mac the window size and VSync. If something still looks wrong, email us; a screenshot helps a lot.
